Practice ran late again. Coach kept us for an extra forty minutes. My legs are dead. We ran drills the entire time. Nobody got to scrimmage. It was brutal.

The bus was late too. I got home at seven. Dinner was cold. Mom saved me a plate. It was meatloaf. I ate it in like five minutes. Then I showered. The hot water ran out. My brother used it all. He takes very long showers. It is annoying.

Homework took an hour. Math was easy. History was boring. I read three chapters. I remember almost nothing. There will be a quiz tomorrow. I am not ready. I never feel ready for quizzes. They are the worst part of school.

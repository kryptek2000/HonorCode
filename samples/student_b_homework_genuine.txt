The Giver is about a town with no pain. There are no choices either. Everything is the same. Everyone follows the rules. It is safe. It is also very empty.

Jonas gets picked for a special job. He learns the truth. The town hides memories. One man holds them all. That man is the Receiver. Jonas trains with him. He feels pain for the first time. He feels joy too. Real joy. Not the fake kind.

The apple scene is important. Jonas sees it change. Nobody else notices. It means he is different. It means he can see beyond. That is why he got picked! I want to know what happens. The ending of chapter three is a cliffhanger. Cliffhangers work. I will keep reading.

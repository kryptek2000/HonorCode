Grandpa visited on Sunday. He brought donuts. He always brings donuts. It is his thing. He told stories about his old truck. That truck is very old. It still runs. He fixes it himself. He is eighty one. He refuses to sell it. Dad wants him to sell it. Grandpa says no every time!

We played cards after lunch. Grandpa won twice. He cheats a little. Everyone knows. Nobody says anything. It is more fun that way. He left at five. The house got quiet. Sundays are good. Family Sundays are the best Sundays.
